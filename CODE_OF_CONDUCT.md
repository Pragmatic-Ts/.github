# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback, welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, and discuss ideas without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexual harassment, spam and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Protect private information and respect intellectual property.

Maintainers may moderate comments, close discussions or restrict participation proportionately to the behavior, with fair review where practical. The rules apply to contribution spaces managed by Pragmatic-Ts.

Use GitHub's reporting controls for platform abuse. For sensitive interpersonal concerns, use an established private maintainer channel; do not post private allegations or personal details publicly. Each product must identify its accountable maintainers and private contact route before opening a managed community.
