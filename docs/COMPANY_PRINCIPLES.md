# Company principles

## Positioning and mission

Pragmatic-Ts builds intelligent business infrastructure: reliable, scalable software that transforms inefficient business operations into intelligent digital systems. The company is product-focused, with primary areas of business SaaS, workflow automation, applied AI, developer infrastructure and selective custom engineering.

The long-term strategy is globally competitive, recurring-revenue software products. Client engineering may fund development, reveal painful recurring problems and build reusable expertise. It is a supporting activity rather than the ultimate business model.

## Principles

1. **Start with a real problem.** Define the user, buyer, existing workflow, pain and evidence before treating an idea as a validated opportunity.
2. **Prefer repeatable value.** Invest in products with recurring use and a credible path to recurring revenue. Do not assume a subscription proves value or retention.
3. **Keep the portfolio open.** Products may serve different industries. Shared standards govern how we build; each product owns what and for whom it builds.
4. **Use intelligence deliberately.** AI must outperform an appropriate simpler baseline or enable a useful capability. Model uncertainty, cost, data rights and failure paths.
5. **Earn reliability.** Treat security, accessibility, operability and maintainability as part of the product, with gates proportionate to risk.
6. **Stay simple.** Prefer clear boundaries, proven tools and small reversible changes. New services, frameworks and abstractions need a concrete benefit.
7. **Record evidence honestly.** Distinguish assumptions, observed facts, implemented behavior, executed checks and live acceptance.
8. **Own the outcome.** Decisions have accountable owners. Automation supports accountability; it does not replace review or product judgment.
9. **Protect trust.** Do not publish customer information, internal economics, credentials or sensitive architecture. Collect only needed data.
10. **Learn across engagements.** Reuse generic patterns only when contracts, intellectual property and privacy allow it. Never move client code or data into a public product without authorization.

## Stable company standards, specific products

Company principles and the engineering constitution change through reviewed decisions. Product requirements live in each product repository and must specify users, validated problem, scope, architecture, roadmap and success criteria. Teams may choose their stack, domain and delivery method within these standards.

New product bets and client engagements use the product evaluation framework. Exceptions to engineering standards require a named owner, rationale, risk, compensating controls and a review date. Unapproved assumptions are not requirements.
